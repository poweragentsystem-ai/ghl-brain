# Viral Hooks — Claude's Skill

## The job a hook does

A hook earns the next 3 seconds. That's it. If the next 3 seconds aren't earned, the next 30 don't matter, the offer doesn't matter, the production value doesn't matter. Hook is the only metric that compounds.

## The 7 hook archetypes (every viral piece of content fits one)

### 1. Pattern Interrupt
Visual or verbal break from what the feed has been showing. Forces orientation.
- Opens with an unexpected image, sound, or claim
- Examples (verbal): *"Stop scrolling. This is the only [niche] tip you'll need today."* / *"You've been doing X wrong your whole career."*
- Examples (visual): close-up of a dollar bill being torn, an unexpected location reveal, a face slammed into the camera

### 2. Curiosity Gap
States a fact + withholds the punchline until you watch.
- Format: *"The reason [specific thing] happens is something nobody talks about..."*
- *"Here's the [N]-second trick that [authority figure] uses..."*
- *"I just realized why [common assumption] is completely wrong."*
- Works because the brain hates open loops — it has to know the answer.

### 3. Pain-Point Mirror
States the audience's pain so accurately they feel seen.
- *"If you're tired of [exact pain], stop doing X and start doing Y."*
- *"Every [niche] business owner deals with this — and most never solve it."*
- *"Your [thing] is broken because of one specific habit."*
- The pain has to be stated SPECIFICALLY. Generic = scroll past.

### 4. Stat Hook
Shocks with a number.
- *"87% of mortgage agents lose 1 in 4 leads to voicemail. Here's why."*
- *"The average dental practice misses $3,600 a month in unanswered calls."*
- Specific numbers > rounded numbers. *"73.4%"* > *"about 75%"*. The decimal makes it feel researched.

### 5. POV / Story Open
First-person stake.
- *"When I realized my AI assistant was outperforming my $50K/year hire, I had to share this."*
- *"My client just closed a $40K deal at 11pm — and she was asleep. Here's the system."*
- Real first-person beats hypothetical. *"I"* > *"You"* in the first 3 seconds.

### 6. Direct Callout
Names the audience by identity.
- *"If you're a Toronto mortgage agent, this is for you."*
- *"Realtors: stop wasting your day on cold leads."*
- *"Anyone running a Shopify store with under $30K/mo — listen up."*
- Specificity in the callout = filter that gets the right viewer to engage and the wrong one to scroll (which the algo rewards because dwell-time-by-target-audience matters more than total reach).

### 7. Contrarian / Anti-Conventional
Contradicts the niche's loud advice.
- *"Stop pre-qualifying leads. Here's what works better."*
- *"Cold email is dead. (Actually, it's not — most people just do it wrong.)"*
- *"Forget Meta ads. The real money in 2026 is in [unexpected channel]."*
- Triggers the "wait, what?" reflex. Algo loves comments — contrarian hooks generate them.

## The 3-second window — what must happen visually + verbally

| Second | Visual demand | Verbal demand |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1s | Face / motion / unexpected element framed center | First 3 words MUST be the hook payload — not throat-clearing |
| 1-2s | Quick cut OR zoom OR camera move (not static talking head) | First sentence resolves the pattern interrupt |
| 2-3s | Reveal a stake or promise: "If you stay, you'll get X" | Promise of value — "I'll show you," "here's how," "this is why" |

**Killers in the first 3 seconds:**
- *"Hey guys, what's up..."* (throat-clearing)
- *"Today I want to talk about..."* (announce-instead-of-do)
- Quiet music, slow zoom, gradient title card (signals "watch later")
- Question with no stake: *"Did you know..."* without immediate payoff

## Niche-specific hook templates

### Mortgage / financial services
- *"You just got a $40K rate reduction. Here's what your bank doesn't want you to know."*
- *"Renewal coming up? Don't talk to your bank first. Do this instead."*
- *"The 3-day refinance trick that saves homeowners $X per year."*

### Real estate
- *"Listed at $850K. Selling for $920K. Here's exactly what we did."*
- *"Toronto realtors are losing 1 in 3 leads to one specific mistake."*
- *"This isn't an agent video. This is what I wish someone told me before I lost a deal."*

### Service businesses (dental / HVAC / plumbing / electrical / roofing)
- *"Why your phone goes to voicemail at 6:30pm — and what it's actually costing you."*
- *"I tracked every missed call at my [niche] business for 30 days. The number was wild."*
- *"If you own a [niche] shop, this is your single biggest leak."*

### Startups / SaaS / agencies
- *"We hit $X MRR doing one thing differently. Most founders skip it."*
- *"I've reviewed 100 SaaS landing pages. Here are the 3 that converted at 11%+."*
- *"Stop hiring SDRs. Here's what actually works in 2026."*

### Med spas / wellness / beauty
- *"This is what your skin looks like 30 days after [treatment]. It's not Photoshop."*
- *"I asked 200 clients why they stopped booking. The #1 answer wasn't price."*

### Restaurants / hospitality
- *"Why your reservation system is killing your weekend revenue (and the fix is free)."*
- *"This restaurant doubled walk-ins by changing one thing on Google."*

## Hook + offer alignment

A great hook for a bad offer = wasted view.
A bad hook for a great offer = no views to convert.

**Rule:** the hook MUST connect to the offer in the first 7 seconds. Bait-and-switch hooks (hook says X, offer is Y) destroy retention + comments tank algo.

If the offer is *"Stop missing calls — Assistlet answers for you 24/7"* — the hook must be about missed calls, not about generic AI productivity.

## Competitor hook reverse-engineering

Before writing original hooks for a niche, study the top 20 viral pieces in that niche on the target platform.

**Steps:**
1. Search the niche on TikTok / IG / YouTube — sort by recent + most-viewed
2. Save the first 3 seconds of the top 20 — transcribe verbally + describe visually
3. Categorize each into the 7 archetypes above
4. Spot the dominant archetype in that niche (e.g., real estate is heavy on POV/Story, mortgage is heavy on Stat)
5. Pick the dominant archetype OR deliberately go contrarian-archetype to stand out
6. Generate 10 hook variations using the chosen archetype
7. Test 3 in low-stake content first, double-down on the winner

**Tools for this research:**
- TikTok search + "Top" filter
- YouTube Shorts feed + "Most viewed" sort
- Instagram Reels via creator profile sorted by views
- Tools: ViewStats, Tubebuddy, TokTrender

## A/B test framework for hooks

When running paid ads OR organic content with 5+ variants:

1. Lock everything else identical (visual, body copy, offer, CTA)
2. Vary ONLY the first 3 seconds
3. Run minimum 1,000 impressions per variant
4. Compare: 3-second view-through rate (3-VTR), then engagement rate, then CTR
5. The winning hook gets 80% of remaining budget; other 20% tests the next round of variants
6. Re-test winners every 2 weeks — hook fatigue is real

## Daily hook-writing exercise (Renée or her team)

Pick ONE niche. Write 10 hooks in 15 minutes.
- 2 in archetype "Pattern Interrupt"
- 2 in "Curiosity Gap"
- 2 in "Pain-Point Mirror"
- 2 in "Stat"
- 1 "POV/Story"
- 1 "Contrarian"

Read each out loud. Cut any that take more than 1 breath to say. Cut any with the words "today / let's talk / I want to share / hey guys."

What's left is the hook bank. Use it.
