# Alex Hormozi — Offers & Leads Playbook (applied)
*Depth file behind the board's Hormozi lens. Load on: offer design, pricing, lead magnets, guarantees, "make this sell." Owner: Kai (Offers) with Mark (Marketing).*

## The Value Equation (every offer gets scored on this)
**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**
- Raise the top: paint the exact after-state; stack proof to raise believed likelihood.
- Shrink the bottom: faster to first result (our "by Friday" hooks do this) and less work ("AI does it" — The Creator's whole pitch).
- Grand Slam Offer test: "so good they feel stupid saying no." If the answer is a shrug, iterate before any traffic spend.

## Offer construction sequence
1. List every problem the buyer hits before/during/after the result.
2. Turn each problem into a solution component (deliverable).
3. Trim to high-value/low-cost-to-deliver components → the STACK (name each, price each, total >> price).
4. Add: guarantee + scarcity/urgency (honest only — ours must never be fake counts) + bonuses (each solves a named objection).

## Guarantees (pick deliberately, never default)
- Unconditional ("30-day any-reason refund") — best for low-ticket (The Creator $17/$47).
- Conditional ("Build-or-Refund if you do X") — best for services/SaaS (Easy Deploy's 30-Day Build-or-Refund).
- Anti-guarantee ("all sales final because…") — premium positioning only.

## Naming: M-A-G-I-C
Magnetic reason-why · Avatar called out · Goal stated · Interval (timeframe) · Container word (challenge/system/blueprint). E.g. "The 5-Day First-Product Sprint."

## $100M Leads — the core four (how strangers find us)
Warm outreach · Content (give away the secrets, sell the implementation — our guide→tool funnel IS this) · Cold outreach · Paid ads. Lead magnets: solve one narrow, urgent problem completely free; the magnet's quality IS the ad for the paid thing.
