# Gary Vaynerchuk — Attention & Content Playbook (applied)
*Depth file behind the board's Vee lens. Load on: organic content, social strategy, hooks, posting cadence. Owner: Mark (Marketing) with Ian (Ideas) + Lex (Copy).*

## Day-trading attention
Attention is the only asset; buy it where it's underpriced. Underpriced now = short-form organic (Reels/TikTok) and unglamorous formats (carousels, text-posts). The feed decides in <1 second — that's why the thumbnail test is non-negotiable.

## Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook
Give, give, give — then ask. Content ratio ≈ 3-4 value posts per 1 promotional post. Our faceless page: teach the digital-product game generously; the funnel link is the occasional right hook. A page that only sells is a page nobody follows.

## Document > Create
Don't manufacture polish — narrate the real process. "Watch me build/test/fail/fix" outperforms staged perfection AND produces content as a byproduct of work we're already doing (The Creator's build itself is a content mine). This is also the cheapest honest content there is — zero invention, zero compliance risk.

## Pillar → Micro model (the volume engine)
One pillar piece (a build session, a long video, a guide chapter) → atomized into 10-30 micro pieces (clips, quote cards, carousels, hooks). Volume with ONE production effort. Our Content Line's Idea station should mostly be fed by atomization, not blank-page brainstorming.

## Platform-native or nothing
The same message is COSTUMED per platform — IG aesthetic, TikTok raw, LinkedIn professional. Cross-posting identical assets reads as spam to every algorithm.

## Speed > perfection, replies = leverage
Post fast, learn from data, double down on what the leaderboard says. First hour replies/comments are free reach multipliers — flag engagement windows to Renée post-launch.

## Where it plugs into our pipeline
- Ideas backlog stocking: atomize pillars first, brainstorm second.
- Ad process STAGE 5 (Vee check): "is this remarkable enough that a stranger stops — or is it wallpaper?"
- Cadence guardrail: capacity-checked (funnel-must-fit-capacity memory) — volume that Renée's approval finger can actually sustain.
