# Viral Video Production — Claude's Skill

Pairs with `viral-hooks.md` (the first 3 seconds) and `ai-video-production.md` (the AI tool stack). This skill is the END-TO-END production system for actually shipping viral-shaped content week after week.

## What "viral" actually means in 2026

- **Viral on TikTok:** 100K+ views, organic, in <72 hours
- **Viral on IG Reels:** 50K+ views, organic, save+share rate >5%
- **Viral on YouTube Shorts:** 250K+ views, AVD >50%
- **Viral on LinkedIn:** 50K+ impressions, comment rate >2%

For most niches, "viral" doesn't mean millions of views — it means **algo amplification 10x your normal**. Hitting that 5-10x lift consistently > one freak millon-view hit.

---

## The 3-input formula

Every viral piece has these three things working together:

1. **Hook that stops the scroll** — see `viral-hooks.md` (7 archetypes)
2. **Niche-specific value** — content the target audience wants to share with others in the niche
3. **Format that the algo currently rewards** — changes every 6-12 weeks per platform

Generic content may get views — but it won't convert and won't compound. Niche-specific viral is what builds revenue.

## Competitor research workflow (mandatory before shooting)

Before producing ANY content for a niche or client:

### Step 1 — Identify top 10 creators in the niche
- Search the niche on TikTok / IG / YouTube
- Sort by recent + view count
- Save handles + follower counts
- Note posting cadence (daily / 3x/week / weekly)

### Step 2 — Sample 50 viral pieces
- Top 5 viral posts from each of the 10 creators (last 90 days)
- Save: hook, format, length, music, caption style, hashtags, comment count, view count

### Step 3 — Pattern extraction
For each viral piece, log:
- Hook archetype (per `viral-hooks.md` 7 categories)
- First-frame visual (what catches the eye?)
- Length (5s / 15s / 30s / 60s+)
- Format (talking head / B-roll over voiceover / split-screen / animation)
- Music (trending track / original audio / silence)
- Caption style (long story / short hook / no caption)
- CTA (subscribe / comment / link / save)

### Step 4 — Find the niche's "winning shape"
After 50 pieces, patterns emerge:
- Mortgage niche on TikTok: usually talking-head + stat hook + 30-60s + trending audio at low volume
- Real estate: usually walkthrough + POV story + 15-30s + native voiceover
- Med spa: usually before/after + transformation reveal + 9-15s + silent

### Step 5 — Pick the dominant shape OR deliberately go contrarian
- Default: produce in the dominant shape (lower risk, faster traction)
- Advanced: deliberately go against the niche pattern to stand out (higher variance, can break out fast)

## Content pillar framework (Gary Vee architecture, public concept)

For any creator / brand running on a content schedule:

### 4 content pillars (mix all 4 each week)

1. **Educational** — teach something specific. Builds authority.
2. **Entertaining** — make them laugh / smile / feel something. Builds reach.
3. **Inspirational** — story / transformation / mission. Builds connection.
4. **Promotional / sales** — soft pitch the offer. Builds revenue.

**Mix ratio:** 4-2-2-1 per week (4 educational + 2 entertaining + 2 inspirational + 1 promotional). Most creators over-promote and tank reach.

### Per pillar, content shapes that work:

**Educational shapes:**
- "3 things I wish I knew before [niche] started"
- "Why [common belief] is wrong"
- "Step-by-step: how I [specific outcome]"
- "Stop doing X. Start doing Y."

**Entertaining shapes:**
- POV (point of view) skits relatable to the niche
- Reaction videos (industry news, viral takes)
- Day-in-the-life
- Trending audio + niche twist

**Inspirational shapes:**
- Origin story / why I started
- Transformation arc (before/after, journey)
- Behind-the-scenes (vulnerability)
- Mission / values reveal

**Promotional shapes:**
- Soft offer at end of value piece
- Customer story / case study
- "What's possible with [offer]" demo
- FAQ / objection handling

## Production rhythm — the weekly cycle

### Sunday — Plan
- Pick 4-7 content topics for the week (mix per pillar ratio)
- Review competitor wins from the past 7 days
- Note 1-2 trending audios to use this week
- Block 2-3 hour shooting windows

### Monday — Shoot batch 1
- Record 3-4 pieces in one session (efficiency)
- Talking-head pieces first (warm up)
- B-roll second (more energy needed)
- Save raw to dated folder

### Tuesday — Edit + post
- Edit Monday's pieces (CapCut, 30-45 min each)
- Post day's piece at peak time (more on this below)
- Schedule remaining for the week

### Wednesday — Engage
- Reply to every comment on yesterday's post within 30 min
- Comment thoughtfully on 10 niche creator posts (community building)
- DMs if anyone asks the offer question

### Thursday — Shoot batch 2
- 3-4 more pieces
- Use anything that emerged from Wednesday's engagement

### Friday — Post + analyze
- Post Friday's piece at peak time
- Run weekly analytics: which post performed best? Why?

### Saturday — Off / batch script ideas for next week

## Posting times by platform (2026 benchmarks)

Adjust per audience timezone + niche pattern. Default starting points:

### TikTok
- Early morning: 6-8 AM (commute scroll)
- Lunch: 12-1 PM (break scroll)
- Prime time: 7-10 PM (couch scroll)

### Instagram Reels
- Morning: 7-9 AM
- Lunch: 12-1 PM
- Evening: 7-9 PM

### YouTube Shorts
- Less time-sensitive — prime is 6-10 PM in the audience's timezone
- Algo cares more about retention than time-of-day

### LinkedIn
- Tuesday-Thursday: 7-9 AM (commute / pre-meeting)
- Avoid weekends

### Niche-specific:
- B2B (mortgage brokerage owners, agency owners): morning + lunch
- Consumer (homeowners, renters, beauty): evening + Sunday
- Trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical owners): early morning + late evening

## Production specs (technical)

### Camera + setup
- iPhone 14 Pro+ or equivalent (modern phone is fine — production gear isn't the bottleneck)
- Tripod or gimbal for stability
- Natural light (window) > ring light > studio light
- Lavalier mic for talking heads (audio quality > video quality)

### Format defaults
- **Vertical 9:16** for TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- **Square 1:1** for IG Feed
- **Horizontal 16:9** for YouTube long-form / LinkedIn

### Resolution
- 1080p minimum (4K only if final delivery requires)
- 30fps standard, 60fps for high-energy / motion-heavy

### Captions
- Burn in for short-form (assume sound-off viewing)
- Use auto-captions in CapCut, manually fix names + numbers
- Brand color background or shadow for readability

### Music / audio
- TikTok: trending audio 50-70% volume
- IG Reels: trending audio OR original + music bed
- YouTube Shorts: original audio + music
- LinkedIn: minimal / no music (B2B audience reads, not listens)

### Editing rhythm
- Cut every 2-3 seconds (motion = retention)
- Zoom punches at emphasis moments
- Text overlays for key points
- Crossfade transitions are AI-builder cheap, jump cuts are 2026-correct

- **Educational:** technique reveals, ingredient sourcing
- **Entertaining:** kitchen chaos, regulars + their orders
- **Inspirational:** opening day story, family business legacy
- **Promotional:** new menu reveal, weekend special

## The "1 in 10" rule

Don't expect every video to viral. Expect 1 in 10 to outperform.

- 7 of 10 will get baseline views (your normal)
- 2 of 10 will under-perform (sub-baseline)
- 1 of 10 will outperform 5-10x (the breakout)

Producing consistently is what gives the algo enough chances to find your breakout. **The creators who quit after 5 videos never see their 1-in-10.** The ones who post 30 in a month see 3 breakouts.

## Post-production rituals

### Within 30 min of posting:
- Reply to first 5 comments (algo loves early engagement)
- Pin the most-replied-to comment (drives more engagement)
- Share to story (cross-platform amplification)

### 24 hours after posting:
- Check view count + engagement rate vs your baseline
- Reply to any new comments
- Save the post if it's outperforming for next-week content reference

### 1 week after posting:
- If it broke out: produce 3-5 follow-up pieces in the same format/topic (ride the wave)
- If it flopped: note what was different from your baseline (less hook, wrong topic, wrong music, wrong time)
