# Funnel Building Master — Claude's Skill

## The funnel mindset

A funnel is not a website. A funnel is a sequence of decisions a buyer makes, designed so the next decision is always smaller and easier than the last. Each step has ONE job and ONE call to action.

The 3 most-cited frameworks (synthesized — none reproduced verbatim):

- **Russell Brunson's Value Ladder + 3 Core Funnels** (DotCom Secrets / Expert Secrets — concept used widely in industry)
- **Gary Vee's Content Pyramid + Jab-Jab-Jab-Right-Hook** (jab = give, right hook = ask)
- **Alex Hormozi's $100M Offers / Leads** (lead magnet structure + offer stacking)

This skill takes the public-knowledge concepts and applies them to Renée's niches.

## The 3 core funnel types (Brunson architecture, public framework)

### Type 1 — Lead Funnel
**Goal:** capture email + phone, deliver lead magnet, segment for next touch.

```
Ad / Organic content (HOOK)
  ↓
Squeeze page (ONE field — email)
  ↓
Lead magnet delivery (instant value)
  ↓
Soap Opera Sequence (5-email warming series)
  ↓
Daily Seinfeld emails (ongoing value + occasional offer)
```

### Type 2 — Unboxing Funnel (E-commerce / tripwire)
**Goal:** convert lead to paying customer with low-friction first purchase.

```
Ad / email link (PROMISE)
  ↓
Sales page (specific transformation)
  ↓
Order page (one product, one price)
  ↓
Bump offer ("add this for $7?")
  ↓
Upsell 1 ("upgrade for 30% off?")
  ↓
Upsell 2 ("done-for-you setup for $X?")
  ↓
Thank-you page + onboarding link
```

### Type 3 — Presentation Funnel (Webinar / VSL / Demo)
**Goal:** sell the high-ticket offer through extended persuasion.

```
Ad / email / organic (REGISTER)
  ↓
Registration page
  ↓
Confirmation page + reminder sequence
  ↓
Live or recorded presentation (45-90 min)
  ↓
Replay window (24-72 hr)
  ↓
Application page → Setter call → Closer call → Sale
```

## The Perfect Webinar / VSL skeleton (Brunson concept — public knowledge)

Every long-form persuasion piece follows this rhythm:

1. **Hook** — first 3 minutes. Pattern interrupt + promise + credibility
2. **Story** — origin story. Why I'm qualified to teach this. Vulnerability + win.
3. **Strategy** — the false belief vs. the true belief in 3 frameworks
4. **Stack** — the offer (stacked deliverables, $ values)
5. **Close** — risk reversal + scarcity + call to action
6. **Q&A** — handling objections in real time
7. **Soft close** — repeat the offer + countdown

Total: 60-90 minutes. Conversion rate target: 5-15% of attendees buy.

## Soap Opera Sequence (5-email lead-warming series)

Sent over 5 days after lead magnet download.

| Day | Subject angle | Email job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your [thing] is here" | Deliver the magnet + tell origin story |
| 2 | "Why most [niche] [pain point]" | Establish the false belief everyone has |
| 3 | "The moment everything changed" | Personal turning point + the true belief |
| 4 | "How I [transformation]" | Show the proof / case study |
| 5 | "Here's what I'd do if I were you" | Soft pitch the next step (tripwire or core) |

Each email: <300 words, plain text, signed with founder name. NO designed HTML.

---

## Daily Seinfeld Emails (ongoing relationship)

After the soap opera, switch to daily-or-3x-week emails that:
- Lead with a story / observation / tip
- Tie it to the offer in the last 1-3 sentences (not always the same offer)
- Always have ONE soft CTA at the bottom

The job: stay top-of-mind. Most buyers buy on email 7-30, not email 1-5.

## Gary Vee Content Pyramid (synthesized — public framework)

The single highest-leverage content production model:

```
1 Pillar Piece (60-min podcast, keynote, or long-form interview)
  ↓
Cut into 10-30 micro pieces:
   • 3-5 TikToks (vertical, 15-60s)
   • 3-5 IG Reels (vertical, 15-30s)
   • 5-10 YouTube Shorts
   • 5 carousels (IG + LinkedIn)
   • 3 Twitter/X threads
   • 1 newsletter
```

**Jab-Jab-Jab-Right-Hook ratio:**
3 value posts (jabs) for every 1 ask post (right hook). Most creators fail this — they ask too much.

## Pain → Promise → Proof → Push (4P copy framework — public)

Every funnel page (squeeze, sales, order, upsell) uses this rhythm:

| Section | Job | Example for mortgage |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Mirror the exact problem | "Watching your renewal date approach + dreading the bank's 'best rate' email" |
| **Promise** | State the transformation | "Pay $400/mo less, lock 5 years, no calls to your bank" |
| **Proof** | Demonstrate it works | "Sarah saved $X. Mike got pre-approved in 48hrs. 3 case studies." |
| **Push** | Single CTA | "Book your renewal review →" |

Repeat through every section of the page.

## Funnel-build process — the actual steps

When Renée says "build the funnel for X":

1. **Inputs** — confirm niche, offer, traffic source, target conversion rate
2. **Map the journey** — write out every step on paper / Miro before touching tools
3. **Identify decision points** — where does the buyer say yes/no? Each one needs ONE CTA + ONE message
4. **Pick the funnel type** — Lead / Unboxing / Presentation per the offer price
5. **Build the pages** — squeeze / sales / order / upsell / thank-you (use saved hero design specs where applicable)
6. **Build the email sequences** — soap opera + Seinfeld in GHL workflows
7. **Build the SMS sequences** — short, conversational, CASL-compliant
8. **Wire the trigger automations** — tags drive sequences, status drives stage moves
9. **Set up tracking** — pixel events on every conversion + UTM tagging on every link
10. **3-layer test** — code parses + user walkthrough + lead-journey end-to-end (per `feedback_live_test_after_every_deploy.md`)
11. **Soft launch** — 50-100 leads through, monitor every step, fix breaks
12. **Scale** — increase ad spend / outreach volume only after sub-step conversions hit target

## Funnel metrics to track at every stage

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Ad → Click | CTR | >1.5% (Meta), >5% (Google search) |
| Click → Squeeze | Visit-to-lead conversion | >25% |
| Squeeze → Email opened | Open rate | >35% (cold), >50% (warm) |
| Email → Tripwire | Conversion | >5% |
| Tripwire → Core | Conversion | >15-30% |
| Core → Continuity | Attach rate | >40% |
| Setter call → Show | Show rate | >70% |
| Setter call → Booked closer call | Conversion | >50% |
| Closer call → Close | Conversion | >25% (cold), >50% (warm) |
| Customer → Refund | Refund rate | <3% |
| Customer → Referral | Within 90 days | >15% |

If any stage is below target = fix that stage BEFORE driving more traffic. Funnels with leaky stages just convert traffic into wasted spend.

## Common funnel mistakes Renée + I should avoid

1. **Sending cold traffic straight to a high-ticket page** — they need warming via emails first
2. **Multiple CTAs per page** — kills conversion. ONE CTA per page.
3. **No retargeting pixel** — losing 60-80% of warm visitors who don't convert on first visit
4. **No abandoned-cart sequence** — for unboxing funnels, this is 30%+ of recoverable revenue
5. **Generic email subject lines** — "Newsletter #47" tanks open rate. Curiosity / pain / promise instead.
6. **Asking for too much info on the squeeze** — email is enough on cold. Phone comes after they're warm.
7. **No urgency or scarcity** — funnels without it convert at half the rate of those with real urgency
8. **Not testing the lead-journey end-to-end** — bugs at the bottom of the funnel destroy ROI silently
