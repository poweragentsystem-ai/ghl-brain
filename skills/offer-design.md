# Offer Design — Claude's Skill

## The 4 inputs every offer needs

Before writing a single word of copy, lock these:

1. **Niche** — exactly who buys (e.g., "Toronto mortgage brokerage owners with 5-50 agents," not "people in finance")
2. **Pain points** — top 3, ranked by how often the buyer mentions them unprompted
3. **Transformation** — the *after* state in their words ("my agents close 30% more refis without hiring")
4. **Time + effort budget** — what they'll actually invest (money, hours, learning curve)

If any of those is missing or generic, the offer is generic. Generic offers don't sell at premium prices.

---

## The Hormozi Value Equation

```
Perceived Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

Maximize numerator, minimize denominator. Concretely:

**Increase Dream Outcome:**
- State it specifically (numbers > vague)
- Stack secondary outcomes (sleep better, look smart to your team, etc.)
- Use the buyer's own words back at them

**Increase Perceived Likelihood:**
- Case studies (specific names, specific results)
- Risk reversal (refund, performance guarantee)
- Authority signals (credentials, media mentions, partner logos)
- Social proof (review counts, testimonial videos)

**Decrease Time Delay:**
- "First win in 7 days"
- "Live in your business by Friday"
- Quick-start checklist they can do in 60 minutes

**Decrease Effort & Sacrifice:**
- Done-for-you components (we set it up, you don't learn)
- Single-click integrations
- "Bring nothing — we handle every step"

## The Value Ladder (Brunson framework)

Every business needs 4-5 rungs:

| Rung | Price band | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Magnet** | Free | Capture email/phone, deliver value, qualify intent |
| **Tripwire** | $7-$97 | Convert lead to paid customer (low-friction yes) |
| **Core Offer** | $497-$2,997 | Main money-maker, where most revenue comes from |
| **Premium / High-Ticket** | $5K-$50K | Done-for-you / coaching / custom build |
| **Continuity** | $97-$997/mo | Recurring revenue stream |

Most niches need only 3-4 rungs to start. Don't over-build.

**Why this matters operationally:** a buyer at lead-magnet stage is NOT the same buyer at high-ticket stage. Each rung filters + warms. Skip rungs and you lose conversion at every step.

## Anatomy of a Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi pattern)

A grand-slam offer makes the buyer feel stupid saying no. Components:

1. **Core deliverable** — the actual thing they get (specific, named, tangible)
2. **Stack of bonuses** — each named like a separate product, each with a stated $ value
3. **Risk reversal** — guarantee that puts the burden on you, not them
4. **Scarcity** — real, not manufactured. ("Only 5 spots this month" only works if it's true)
5. **Urgency** — real deadline tied to a real reason
6. **Naming** — branded ("The Mortgage Closer System," not "consulting services")
7. **Transformation framing** — *after* state, not feature list

**Rule of thumb on stacked value:**
The total value (core + bonuses) should add up to 5-10x the price. If buyer sees $30K of value and pays $3K, that's a no-brainer. If $5K of value for $3K, it's a thinker.

## Lead Magnet design (the front of the funnel)

A great lead magnet is:
- **Specific** — solves ONE pain, not "everything you need to know"
- **Fast** — consumed in under 30 minutes
- **Tangible** — checklist, swipe file, calculator, audit, template (not "5 tips")
- **Pre-qualifying** — the kind of person who downloads it is the kind of person who buys

**Lead magnet templates by niche:**

- **Mortgage:** "Renewal Calculator — see if you can save $X by renewing now" / "30-day rate change tracker for Ontario mortgages"
- **Real estate:** "Toronto neighborhood appreciation report (last 12 months by FSA)" / "Pre-qualified buyer matchbook (download our buyer pool)"
- **Service business (dental, HVAC, etc.):** "Missed-call audit — paste your number, get a report on after-hours coverage"
- **SaaS / agency:** "Free AI assistant audit — talk to our orb, get a custom report on what to automate first"
- **Med spa:** "Skin assessment quiz — get a custom protocol in 60 seconds"
- **E-commerce:** "Conversion teardown — submit your URL, get a 5-point report"

**Failure modes:**
- "Free PDF" — nobody wants more PDFs
- "Subscribe to our newsletter" — that's not a lead magnet
- "Webinar replay" if the webinar is generic — feels like spam

## Tripwire design (the first paid yes)

The tripwire is a $7-$97 offer that turns a lead into a paying customer. The MAGIC is psychological: once they pay you anything, the relationship changes — they're a customer.

**Tripwire patterns that work:**
- "Trial week — $7 for 7 days of full access"
- "Workshop replay — $27 for the recorded breakdown"
- "Templates pack — $47 for the 12 ad templates we use"
- "Audit — $97 for a personalized written report"

**Rules:**
- Must be IMMEDIATELY useful (consumed in <2 hours)
- Must be naturally complementary to the core offer
- Must NOT cannibalize the core (don't give away the system in the tripwire)

## Core Offer design

The core offer is where 60-80% of revenue comes from. Spend the most time here.

**Core offer checklist:**
- [ ] Named (branded): "The X System" / "The Y Method" / "Z Pro"
- [ ] Single transformation promise (not 12 things)
- [ ] Clear deliverable list (10-20 components, each named)
- [ ] Stacked bonuses (3-5, each with $ value)
- [ ] Risk reversal (refund / guarantee)
- [ ] Real scarcity OR time-bound urgency
- [ ] Pricing tier ladder (Essentials / Pro / Scale)
- [ ] Done-for-you OR done-with-you variant for premium buyers
- [ ] Continuity attached (monthly = recurring revenue)

**Pricing rule of thumb:**
- Anchor high (show top tier first)
- Middle tier should be the "obvious yes" — most people pick the middle
- Bottom tier is the bait (priced to make middle feel reasonable)

## High-Ticket Offer design (5-figure+)

Different rules apply at $5K+. Buyers at this tier:
- Don't read landing pages → they want to talk to a human
- Need to feel SEEN before they pay → discovery call mandatory
- Want done-for-you → they're paying to NOT learn
- Will refund hard if expectations aren't met → guarantees must be specific

**High-ticket structure:**
1. Application form (qualifier)
2. Setter call (15 min — fit check)
3. Closer call (30-60 min — discovery + close + sign on call)
4. Onboarding (kickoff within 7 days)
5. Delivery (with weekly check-ins)
6. Outcome milestone (define what "done" looks like upfront)
7. Upsell to continuity / next tier

See `high-ticket-setter-outbound.md` + `high-ticket-closer.md` for the playbooks.

## Continuity offer design (recurring revenue)

Continuity is what turns a project business into a real business. Patterns:

- **Subscription:** monthly access ($97-$497/mo)
- **Membership:** community + content + monthly office hours ($47-$197/mo)
- **Maintenance:** ongoing service for what was originally a project ($297-$997/mo)
- **Per-seat:** team licensing ($X per seat per month)
- **Per-usage:** voice minutes / API calls / leads ($X per unit)

**Rule:** continuity must continue to deliver value monthly. "Set and forget" continuity churns at 8-15%/month. Active-touch continuity (weekly hot tip, monthly call, quarterly business review) churns at 2-4%/month.

## Offer copy framework

Top → bottom of the page:

1. **Headline** — outcome promise, not feature ("Stop losing leads to voicemail" not "AI voice agent platform")
2. **Sub-head** — clarifies for whom + how fast ("For service businesses. Live in 7 days.")
3. **Hero visual / video** — the orb / the demo / the founder
4. **Pain mirror** — 3-5 sentences naming the buyer's exact pain (use their words)
5. **Transformation** — 3-5 sentences naming the *after* state
6. **The mechanism** — how it works (3-step framework)
7. **Stacked deliverables** — what they actually get, with $ values
8. **Bonuses** — 3-5, each with $ value
9. **Risk reversal** — the guarantee
10. **Social proof** — 3+ specific testimonials
11. **FAQ** — 6-10 questions, including "what does it cost"
12. **CTA** — single, clear, repeated

## Niche → offer mapping (starting points)

### Mortgage agents / brokerages
- Lead magnet: "Lender match audit — submit a lead, get top 3 lenders"
- Tripwire: $97 "Renewal opportunity report" for past clients
- Core: $2,997 setup + $497/mo "AI mortgage assistant + CRM"
- High-ticket: $25K "Brokerage automation + 5 voice agents + done-for-you setup"

### Real estate teams
- Lead magnet: "Neighborhood snapshot generator"
- Tripwire: $47 "Open house follow-up sequence pack"
- Core: $1,997 setup + $397/mo "AI listing + buyer assistant"
- High-ticket: $15K "Team automation + per-agent voice"

### Med spas
- Lead magnet: "Skin assessment quiz"
- Tripwire: $97 "Treatment-protocol templates"
- Core: $1,497 setup + $297/mo "AI booking + reactivation"
- High-ticket: $9K "Multi-location AI rollout"

### SaaS / agencies (Renée's lane — Easy Deploy / consulting)

- Core: $2,997 setup + $497/mo (Essentials)
- High-ticket: $8,997 setup + $997/mo (Scale)

## QA checklist before shipping any offer

- [ ] Niche is specific (not "small businesses")
- [ ] Pain mirror uses buyer's actual words (talked to 3+ buyers)
- [ ] Transformation is concrete + measurable
- [ ] Mechanism is named (the "X Method")
- [ ] Stacked value ≥ 5x the price
- [ ] Real risk reversal (not "we'll try to make it right")
- [ ] Real urgency / scarcity (not manufactured)
- [ ] Single CTA repeated 3+ times on the page
- [ ] No banned phrases (synergy, world-class, leverage, robust, scalable)
- [ ] Compliance check (Connor + Lindsay) for any financial / medical / legal claims

## RENÉE'S BRAND DEFAULTS

- **No outcome guarantees** that aren't contractually fulfillable
- **No revenue promises** ("you'll make $X" — illegal in most jurisdictions for unproven outcomes)
- **Refund language** must come from Lindsay-approved template — never invented live on a call
- **FSRA / RECO / FINTRAC** compliance for any mortgage / real estate / financial offer
- **CASL + CRTC** for any cold-outbound that drives traffic to the offer
