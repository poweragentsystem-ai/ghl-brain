# Russell Brunson — Funnels & Story Playbook (applied)
*Depth file behind the board's Brunson lens. Load on: funnels, landing pages, ad diagnosis, story copy. Owner: Mark (Marketing) with Lex (Copy).*

## Hook · Story · Offer (the diagnostic — memorize)
Anything underperforming fails at one of exactly three points:
- **Hook** — did it stop the scroll? (thumbnail test, first line, first second of video)
- **Story** — did it hold attention and build belief? (Epiphany Bridge: backstory → wall → discovery → result → transformation. Feel it, don't explain it.)
- **Offer** — did it make saying yes easy? (stack, guarantee, urgency — see hormozi-offers.md)
Diagnose in that order. Never rebuild an offer when the hook is what's broken.

## Value Ladder
Free (lead magnet) → low-ticket ($17 guide) → core ($47 tool) → premium (Easy Deploy tiers / consulting). Every property must know its rung and ASCEND: each delivery experience sells the next rung (our thank-you-page ascension). A business with one rung leaves most revenue on the table.

## Funnel selection by rung
- Lead funnel (squeeze page): free thing for an email.
- Unboxing/low-ticket: sales page → order bump → 1-2 upsells (our live $17 → +$9 bump → $47 = textbook).
- Presentation (VSL/webinar): mid-ticket, belief-shifting needed.
- Application/phone: high-ticket (Easy Deploy setup slider, consulting).

## Traffic temperature dictates the ad
- HOT (knows us): talk product. WARM (knows the problem): talk solution. COLD (knows nothing): talk THEM — enter the conversation already in their head. Cold ads that talk product die; most of our IG traffic is COLD → hooks must be pain/desire-first, never feature-first.

## One Funnel Rule
One funnel, optimized relentlessly, beats five half-built ones. (Priority-stack gate says the same thing.)

## Where it plugs into our pipeline
- Ad process STAGE 2: tag every hook HOT/WARM/COLD and check it matches the audience temperature of the placement.
- Funnel reviews: run Hook-Story-Offer on each page section before shipping.
- Performance triage: leaderboard losers get diagnosed hook→story→offer, in that order, before regeneration.
